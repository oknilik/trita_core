## What do the team and its leader see?

After completing the assessment, each participant sees only their own personality profile. The consultant-approved report prepared for the team and its leader contains an aggregate team picture: team averages across the dimensions, bands showing internal diversity, and a shared interpretation. It contains no named rows and does not show any other team member's individual score.

<Callout>
The aggregate team profile does not reveal where any person stands. It helps the team discuss shared working situations instead of speculating about individuals.
</Callout>

Each member's own view is more personal: they can compare their values with the team's aggregate picture. That comparison applies only to them. It does not provide access to colleagues' profiles.

## What do the team average and internal diversity mean?

**The team average** is one starting point for the shared profile. A higher average on Conscientiousness, for example, may suggest that planning and consistent execution matter in how the team works. It does not mean that everyone works in the same way or that the team is inflexible in every situation. The report's interpretation can instead raise the question of whether enough room remains for replanning when circumstances change.

Similarly, with a higher average on Agreeableness, it is worth examining whether uncomfortable professional disagreements still have room within otherwise smooth collaboration. A higher Extraversion average does not identify who speaks more or less; it may direct attention to whether quick verbal responses leave enough space for ideas developed in writing or with more preparation time.

**The internal-diversity band** indicates how similar or varied the response patterns are within the aggregate picture. A wider band may reflect several ways of working, but the report deliberately does not show who sits at any point in that band. The shared question is therefore not "who is the outlier?" but in which working situations this diversity may matter.

This is particularly important for sensitive dimensions such as Honesty-Humility. The consultant's interpretation may open a conversation about fairness and visible or less visible work, but it cannot support conclusions about any person's intentions or integrity.

<KeyInsight>
The aggregate report does not show what each person is like. It gives the team shared language for examining how it works in concrete situations.
</KeyInsight>

## From report to working agreement

The consultant-approved interpretation becomes useful when the team compares it with its own experience. The workshop does not try to decode individual scores. It examines observable situations, such as how decisions are made, when people can contribute with enough preparation, or where planning becomes cumbersome.

These conversations can lead to a working agreement the team can test. For example, the team might introduce written input before decisions and then review at an agreed time whether it helped. The facilitated process complements individual development, coaching, and leadership observation rather than replacing them.

## Sources and limitations

Alongside the profile, a team's functioning is shaped by the task, leadership, role clarity, and the team's history. The aggregate report offers hypotheses, not a diagnosis. The associations found in research are moderate and context-dependent; there is no universally ideal composition. Team averages and diversity bands are also subject to measurement uncertainty, and a 0-100 display is not a percentile. The precise meaning of each dimension label is fixed by the scale's documentation - Emotionality, for example, is not simply reversed neuroticism.

- [Peeters et al.: meta-analysis of personality and team performance](https://doi.org/10.1002/per.588)
- [Bell: meta-analysis of deep-level team composition and performance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17484544/)
- [Van Dijk et al.: registered meta-analysis on personality diversity](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10869-024-09977-0)
- [Official HEXACO scale descriptions](https://hexaco.org/scaledescriptions)

*These studies describe average, group-level associations. They do not allow confident prediction of a specific team member's behaviour or a team's future performance. Other people's individual results cannot be reliably inferred from the report, nor is that the purpose of shared interpretation.*
