## Everyone knows MBTI

INTJ. ENFP. INFJ. These four-letter codes have been in use for decades, and for many people they provide a shared language for self-discovery. The problem is not that MBTI is worthless – it's that it is good for something different from what many people use it for.

<Callout>
MBTI is a doorway into self-awareness. TRITAN is a measuring instrument. There's no point comparing the two – better to understand what each is for.
</Callout>

## What is predictive validity?

Predictive validity measures whether a test accurately forecasts real behaviour. If a test says someone is conscientious, do they actually deliver accurately and on time?

For MBTI, research generally finds no strong link between types and actual workplace performance. In the six-factor model, the <DimBadge code="THOR" label="Thoroughness" /> and <DimBadge code="INTE" label="Integrity" /> dimensions are strong predictors of both job performance and ethical behaviour.

## MBTI's core problem: the dichotomy

MBTI places people along I/E, N/S, T/F and J/P axes. The problem: in reality these are not categorical differences – they are continuous scales.

<KeyInsight>
If someone is "just barely" I or "just barely" E near the boundary, a small measurement error is enough to land them in the other category 5 weeks later – without anything changing in them at all.
</KeyInsight>

TRITAN, by contrast, measures each dimension on a 0–100 scale. An extraversion score of 52 differs from one of 48 – but the difference is a nuance, not a categorical jump.

## The unique TRITAN dimension: Integrity

<DimBadge code="INTE" label="Integrity" /> is the one dimension that neither MBTI nor the classic Big Five model includes. It measures:

- The absence of manipulative behaviour
- Avoidance of self-serving moves and ethical shortcuts
- Low levels of grandiosity and status orientation

Research shows the integrity (honesty-humility) factor is one of the strongest predictors of workplace deviance and ethical decision-making – in both directions.

## When to use which?

MBTI works well for team-building workshops where the goal is a shared language and better mutual understanding – not predictive measurement.

TRITAN belongs where accuracy matters: development-focused self-awareness, observer comparison, research.

The two don't compete – each is valuable in a different context.


*Methodological note: TRITAN is Trita's implementation of the six-factor personality model family; the research findings cited in this article refer to the six-factor model family.*
