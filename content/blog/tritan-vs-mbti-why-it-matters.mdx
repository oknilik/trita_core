## Two frameworks built for different purposes

Many people know MBTI. Its four-letter codes are memorable and can provide an accessible vocabulary for reflection. HEXACO, by contrast, describes individual differences across six continuous personality dimensions.

<Callout>
The useful question is not which label sounds better. It is what decision the assessment is meant to support, and whether the specific instrument has evidence for that use.
</Callout>

Both approaches can use self-report, so both can be influenced by response style and social desirability. It is inaccurate to claim that one measures an ideal self while the other directly measures actual behaviour.

## What predictive validity means

Predictive validity describes the association between an assessment result and a relevant criterion observed later. It does not mean that a score can determine how a specific person will behave.

In one meta-analysis, HEXACO <DimBadge code="H" label="Honesty–Humility" /> showed a meaningful association with counterproductive work behaviour and a much smaller association with task performance. These are probabilistic group-level findings – not an individual “ethics test”.

## Type or continuous dimension?

An important limitation of MBTI is not that every underlying score is necessarily unreliable. It is that continuous differences are converted into letter categories. Near a boundary, a small score change can alter one letter of the type even when the person's functioning has not changed meaningfully.

<KeyInsight>
A dimensional result preserves more nuance, but a difference between 48 and 52 is interpretable only when the scale's norms and measurement uncertainty are known.
</KeyInsight>

trita's 0–100 display is a reporting transformation, not an inherent feature of the HEXACO model. A number alone does not make a measure more precise or valid.

## HEXACO's distinctive addition

<DimBadge code="H" label="Honesty–Humility" /> is HEXACO's most distinctive additional domain. It is not a separate broad factor in the classic Big Five, although some of its content overlaps with aspects of Agreeableness. The official HEXACO framework describes four facets: sincerity, fairness, greed avoidance, and modesty.

A low score does not prove lying, manipulation, or unethical conduct. It should never be used as a personal verdict or exclusion rule and can only be interpreted alongside concrete behavioural evidence.

## When to use which

MBTI can offer an accessible conversation framework in a reflective or team workshop. That does not establish that it improves team performance or that it is suitable for role allocation or selection.

HEXACO has a stronger research base as a personality model. Even so, each questionnaire must separately demonstrate that its translation, items, scoring, and intended use are reliable and valid.
